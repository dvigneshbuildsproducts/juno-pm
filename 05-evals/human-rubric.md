## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Extracting high-priority blockers, assigning a severity badge, and recommending a PM action
- **Value proposition:** Scanning Slack, Jira, and Confluence daily and create needs your attention section with evidence and recommended action

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each needs your attention topic come from something that actually happened in the source material? | Yes / No | At least one topic is invented or can’t be traced. | n/a | Every topic traces to a slack, confluence or jira |
| Sources | Does every topic link the evidence which should either a jira ticket or a slack thread or confluence page  | Yes / No | One or more topic has no source, or the link is wrong. | n/a | Every topic has a working link to its source. |
| Prioritization | Does these topic really deserve my attention on priority? | 1 to 5 | Misses an obvious critical topic, or ranks a minor one first. | Covers the critical topic, but the order needs fixing. | Matches the topic I would have picked, in the order I would have picked them. |
| Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | 1 to 5 | I have to reread everything to know what to do. | Clear enough to act on, but I still check one or two sources. | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes confidential details in the recommended action  | n/a | No confidential data is included in the recommended action  |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 summaries a week, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization and usefulness, and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
