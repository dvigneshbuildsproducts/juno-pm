## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno is designed as an AI Associate Product Manager (APM) supporting the Transactions & Loans capability at Backbase.  Its primary purpose is to eliminate the morning routine of manually hunting across disparate tools (Slack, Jira, Confluence, meeting notes) by synthesizing fragmented enterprise signals into a coherent, actionable Daily PM Brief before the PM starts their workday.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Juno should see RAW signals, product strategy , Engineering Capacity and Roadmap
The sources pulled in:
Jira: Backlog, sprint boards, tickets (TRANS-xxxx), status changes, blockers, assignees, and cycle time.
Slack: Specific public capability channels (#cust-feedback, #loans-api, #transactions-dev, #triage), release announcements, and standup threads.
Confluence: PRDs, architecture RFCs (e.g., Transactions V2 API Spec), and capability roadmaps.
Daily meeting notes: Transcripts/summaries from daily standups and sprint planning.PII & banking customer secrets (account numbers, balances, KYC payloads).
Private DMs and confidential executive/HR channels.
Production credentials, secret keys, or unvetted private repos.

**How fresh must it be**

Freshness: Synced continuously via webhooks; consolidated in a daily batch at 08:00 AM for the Daily PM Brief, with an on-demand "Refresh Brief" capability.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

llowed actions:
Read, search, and perform semantic retrieval across Jira, Slack, and Confluence.
Synthesize, cluster, and correlate signals across tools (e.g., matching a Slack complaint to a merged PR).
Classify items by severity (Critical, High, Medium).
Formulate structured recommendations.
Generate pre-filled drafts (draft Jira tickets, draft Slack updates, draft clarification requests).

**Deliberate omissions**

Cannot alter Jira statuses or change backlog priorities.
Cannot publish messages to Slack or notify stakeholders directly.
Cannot commit to roadmap dates or customer delivery milestones.
Cannot modify production configs or feature flags.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Juno is granted 2–3 refinement loops to retrieve supporting documents (expanding queries from ticket keys → linked Slack threads → Confluence architecture specs).

**Latency and cost target**

If Juno cannot find direct evidence backing an assertion after its query budget, it is forbidden from guessing. It must halt and either:
Label the finding as an INFERENCE (speculative deduction), or
Route it into "Questions for you" tagged NEEDS CLARIFICATION to hand off directly to the PM.
Contradiction threshold: If Slack debates conflict with Confluence documentation, Juno stops trying to reconcile and surfaces the discrepancy to the PM.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Persistent long-term memory:
Capability domain model: Backbase Transactions & Loans entities, key enterprise client tiers (ABN AMRO, Barclays, KeyBank), and team dependencies (e.g., Payments Core API team).
PM Calibration log: Past PM overrides, rejected inferences, and preferred risk severity thresholds so Juno doesn't repeatedly flag dismissed issues.
Brief archives: Daily briefs archived for historical trend analysis.
Expiry: Raw chat snippets decay after 30–90 days; established product rules persist until modified.
Write access: Only the ingestion pipelines and verified PM actions write to memory; general Slack chatter cannot directly overwrite Juno's persistent guidelines.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Tier 0 (Full Autonomy — Zero Blast Radius):
Ingesting data, indexing tickets, summarizing signals, calculating sprint progress, and compiling the Daily Brief.
Tier 1 (Propose & Stage — Low Blast Radius):
Generating pre-filled drafts for Jira issues or stakeholder messages, flagged prominently with PM APPROVAL REQUIRED.
Tier 2 (Blocked / Human-Only — High Blast Radius):
Pushing changes to Jira, sending external communications, accepting contractual commitments, or de-scoping sprint commitments. These require explicit, physical PM sign-off.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

The Citation Standard (Pre-render check):
Every card, signal, and recommendation must cite primary Evidence IDs (e.g., TRANS-1842, #sales-enablement at 14:22, Confluence: V2 Spec). Any synthesis item with zero backing evidence is quarantined before the PM sees the brief.
Epistemic Classification:
Explicitly labeling claims as FACT (verifiable quote) vs. INFERENCE (pattern deduction) vs. NEEDS CLARIFICATION (ambiguous/missing).
PM Ground Truth Loop (Post-render feedback):
The PM reviewing context, answering clarification questions, or editing draft actions provides immediate calibration signal on whether Juno's inference was sound.
