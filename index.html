Module 3
AI PRD
Problem and user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno is designed as an AI Associate Product Manager (APM) supporting the Transactions & Loans capability at Backbase. Its primary purpose is to eliminate the morning routine of manually hunting across disparate tools (Slack, Jira, Confluence, meeting notes) by synthesizing fragmented enterprise signals into a coherent, actionable Daily PM Brief before the PM starts their workday.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

Permissions

The permission tier

| Side-effect class | Tier |
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

Justification

Tier 0 (Full Autonomy — Zero Blast Radius):
Ingesting data, indexing tickets, summarizing signals, calculating sprint progress, and compiling the Daily Brief.
Tier 1 (Propose & Stage — Low Blast Radius):
Generating pre-filled drafts for Jira issues or stakeholder messages, flagged prominently with PM APPROVAL REQUIRED.
Tier 2 (Blocked / Human-Only — High Blast Radius):
Pushing changes to Jira, sending external communications, accepting contractual commitments, or de-scoping sprint commitments. These require explicit, physical PM sign-off.

Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Synthesizing fragmented enterprise signals into a coherent, actionable Daily PM Brief before the PM starts their workday

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Ingesting data, indexing tickets, summarizing signals, calculating sprint progress, and compiling the Daily Brief Juno has full permission.

Pushing changes to Jira, sending external communications, accepting contractual commitments, or de-scoping sprint commitments. These require explicit, physical PM sign-off.

Access Control:

Juno have access to only the transactions and loans slack channels and the Jira projects related to loans and transactions.Juno has access to the customer confluence page and capability confluence page.

Fallback Protocols:

After 3 failed retrieval attempts from Jira, Slack, or Confluence, stop retrying that source and continue with available sources. Clearly flag the missing source in the Daily PM Brief. After 2 consecutive tool/API errors, stop the workflow and escalate to the PM with the error, failed step, and execution trace. Never invent missing information.

Checkpoints:

Require PM review before publishing any high-impact customer escalation, legal, security, compliance, or P0/P1 issue. Medium-confidence findings require PM review before publishing. Low-confidence findings must not be published and require PM approval or clarification. High-confidence routine updates can be published automatically.

Module 6

How I’ll know it’s working

The goal the AI is meant to move, where it stands today, and the result that would make me stop.

The goal

Cross-team dependency risks and technical blockers are identified and escalated within 24 hours of their first appearance across Slack and Jira, rather than surfacing during end-of-sprint retrospectives or post-incident escalations helping to deliver features on time.

Baseline

Currently, ~26% of committed sprint story points slip across sprints because blockers sit unaddressed in fragmented Slack threads or stale tickets for an average of 3.2 days before reaching the PM.

Target and review date

Reduce sprint spillover to under 12% within 3 sprint cycles, cutting average blocker detection latency to < 24 hours.

Usage metric

PM Engagement Rate on Critical/High Risks (percentage of flagged risks where the PM opens the Evidence Drawer, creates an action draft, or takes a logged mitigation).

Pattern I’ll watch for first

Alert fatigue / passive ignoring. If the PM leaves >40% of flagged risks unreviewed for 48+ hours, or rapidly dismisses them without viewing source evidence, Juno’s sensitivity threshold is set too high (crying wolf), generating noise instead of high-conviction signal.

Stop line

We will stop using Juno's risk scanner if:
>25% of flagged High/Critical risks are false alarms (violating the accuracy deal-breaker: risks invented or ungrounded in primary sources), OR
A critical production blocker or enterprise client commitment slips that was openly discussed in monitored Slack/Jira channels but Juno failed to detect.

Reflection

What I learned

Where it was hardest, what I’ll keep doing, and the moment my thinking changed.

Friction

Shifting my mental model from 'one prompt → one response' to stateful loops (monitoring, action drafts, and human calibration feedback) required unlearning traditional product specs.

Key learnings

How to create high quality prompt, skill file, the harness especially the looping was new for me

Aha moment

Realizing my role isn't obsolete in an AI-native world—it evolves from doing manual synthesis and triage to architecting the system. As a PM, my core job becomes writing the evaluation rubrics, setting governance boundaries (blast radius tiers), and designing the calibration loops that make the agent trustworthy
